Privacy Policy

How Navigo handles translation content and purchase information.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Terms of Use Privacy Policy Support

Overview

Navigo performs speech recognition, text and photo translation, and optional voice generation on your device after the relevant models are downloaded. We do not operate an account for your translation content or receive that content for processing. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchase and subscription information separately, as described below.

Translation content

Navigo does not send your recordings, transcripts, translations, photos, photo-library content, or voice profiles to us, Apple, or RevenueCat for translation. We do not use this content for advertising or analytics. If you choose to share or export content through your device, the destination you select may receive it.

After the necessary models are ready, translation and recognition can run without a network connection. Model downloads, purchases, purchase restoration, and subscription status checks require network access.

Local storage and deletion

Recordings, generated audio, translation history, voice profiles, and downloaded models are stored in the App's local storage on your device. We do not provide cloud backup for this content. You can remove local history, voice profiles, and downloaded models through the controls available in Navigo. Deleting the App removes its local data from the device, subject to your device's backup settings and operating-system behavior. Keep copies of important content before deleting it; we cannot recover content stored only on your device.

Model downloads

When you request an offline model, Navigo connects to a model host or mirror to download it. The host may receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address and request metadata, under its own policies. The downloaded model is stored on your device. Translation content is not sent to the model host for processing.

Device permissions

  • Microphone: records speech for translation or an optional reference voice for voice cloning.
  • Camera: captures images for photo translation.
  • Photo Library: lets you choose an image to translate or save a translated image when you request that action.

These inputs are processed on your device. You can change or revoke permissions in iOS Settings; the related feature may stop working if a required permission is revoked.

Purchases and subscription data

Apple processes App Store payments, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and purchase history through your Apple Account. Navigo uses RevenueCat to display available products and verify, restore, and maintain Navigo Pro access. For those purposes, RevenueCat may process an App-specific user identifier, product and transaction identifiers, subscription or entitlement status, and technical request information. We do not send your recordings, photos, translations, or voice profiles to RevenueCat.

Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase information under their own policies and legal obligations. Deleting Navigo does not erase Apple purchase records or cancel an active subscription. We do not use purchase information for advertising. For questions or requests about purchase-related information, contact onebestai@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Navigo is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their translation content. Purchases, if any, are subject to Apple Account and family controls. Contact us if you have a concern about a child's purchase-related information.

Security

Your translation content remains in the iOS app sandbox and is protected by your device's security controls. Protect your device with a passcode or biometric lock. Purchase and entitlement checks use network connections to Apple and RevenueCat. We do not provide cloud storage for your recordings, photos, translations, or voice profiles.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Navigo or applicable requirements change. We will publish the revised policy here and update the date above. Material changes will be made available in the App where appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email onebestai@gmail.com. We cannot retrieve or delete translation content that exists only on your device.
