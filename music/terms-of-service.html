4. Transfers, rooms and connections

  • Reachable network: local transfers, capsules, bump sharing and rooms require a reachable network or a connection to one device’s hotspot. Bluetooth discovery alone does not establish a file-transfer network. There is currently no relay between arbitrary public-internet devices.
  • Trusted recipients: a paired browser can read available track information and download managed audio. Capsule links contain temporary credentials. Share them only with your intended recipients, not publicly.
  • Security: local HTTP is not end-to-end encrypted. Use a trusted network and stop sharing, close the room or disable bump sharing afterward. Stopping a service does not recall previously saved files.
  • Playback synchronization: rooms exchange audio, state and clock information. Speakers, Bluetooth headphones, scheduling and network conditions affect actual latency. We do not promise zero delay or a fixed millisecond acoustic alignment on every device.
  • Hotspots and carriers: supported Android devices can create a local-only hotspot. Other hotspot operations may depend on system settings or carrier support. Backgrounding, locking or resource restrictions may interrupt transfers.

5. Files, deletion and backups

Backup, restore and migration depend on your selections, readable sources and available space. Before cleanup, uninstalling or changing devices, save a backup outside the app and verify that it opens and contains important audio. We do not hold a cloud copy from which to recover your local media.

Do not uninstall or clear data solely to troubleshoot before considering a backup or contacting support. Check the trustworthiness of third-party files you choose to import, download or share.
