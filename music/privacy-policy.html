1. Scope, provider and contact

This policy covers Music (Local Music) on iOS, iPadOS, macOS and Android, including versions distributed through Google Play, and related support on this website. Music is maintained and published under the Manna Studio brand. The maintenance team handles privacy inquiries at onebestai@gmail.com. The store product page identifies the developer distributing your specific version; we will update this policy if the provider changes.

It describes both on-device processing and connections you choose. Features differ by hardware, platform, distribution channel and version; listing a feature here does not enable it on your device. “Local-first” does not mean that data never leaves your device or that you have agreed to every optional transfer.

2. Information stored on your device

To provide playback and library management, Music processes and stores the following locally:

  • Media and file information: imported or scanned audio, video, lyrics, artwork, filenames, source locations, sizes, durations, tags, and file fingerprints used to identify duplicate content. Some import methods create managed media copies in app storage.
  • Library and preferences: playlists, favorites, playback history, queue and position, edited lyric copies, sound settings, language, scan filters, other preferences, and import results and failure reasons.
  • Folder access: persistent Android directory grants, Apple security-scoped bookmarks, and associated paths to revisit folders you select. They do not give us access to other apps’ private directories.
  • External source settings: the service name, address, share path, username and password you enter, if any, are saved in the local app database. This is not a dedicated encrypted password vault; use a separate account with limited permissions.
  • System media display: track titles, artists, artwork and playback state are passed to system media services for lock-screen controls, notifications and widgets. Apple widgets can read a playback snapshot through a local App Group. Lock-screen visibility depends on your system settings.

Core playback and library management do not require an account. We do not automatically upload your library to the maintenance team’s servers. The app includes no advertising, profiling, third-party analytics or automatic crash-upload SDK.

Media and tags may include your or another person’s voice, name, image, location descriptions or other private information. Music treats them as media you choose to manage, not as a basis for health, identity or interest profiling. Check contents before sharing. Content fingerprints support matching and deduplication, not cross-app advertising identification.

7. Advertising, tracking, sale and automated decisions

Music currently has no behavioral advertising, advertising-identifier tracking or cross-app/website tracking. We do not sell or rent personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, profile local media for marketing or use it to train AI models. Library sorting, filtering, deduplication and playback synchronization do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

We do not penalize the exercise of privacy rights with discriminatory prices or reduced basic service, and offer no financial incentive conditioned on selling personal information. Where US state privacy rules on sale, sharing or targeted advertising apply, we currently conduct no such activity to opt out of; you can still stop optional transfers and submit rights requests.

The app does not track browsing using cookies, fingerprinting or hidden identifiers. Apple or Google may separately process system diagnostics, backups, store transactions or account information under your settings and their policies. That is distinct from Music automatically uploading your library to the developer.

8. Security and sharing boundaries

Local connections use session tokens, pairing codes or temporary links to restrict access. Current local HTTP transfers do not provide TLS or end-to-end encryption. Pairing codes, temporary links and content may be exposed to interception on an untrusted network. Use a trusted Wi-Fi network or hotspot and do not expose the service through router port forwarding.

Browser transfers and capsules use temporary sessions that normally expire after 20 minutes and can be stopped sooner. Expiry does not delete files already sent, copies saved by recipients, browser downloads or exported backups. Leave or close a listening room to end it; turn off bump sharing to stop that feature.

WebDAV over HTTP also lacks transport encryption for authentication. Prefer a trusted HTTPS service and a limited-permission account. We do not promise absolute security for networks or the local database.

We limit temporary links by scope and lifetime and use system sandboxing, access grants, pairing and session validation to reduce unauthorized access. These controls do not change the current use of cleartext HTTP or represent independent security certification. If we learn of a personal-data security incident involving information we actually hold, we will take reasonable response measures and notify affected people or regulators when required by applicable law.

9. Support, diagnostics and these webpages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and the device details, screenshots or attachments you choose to provide. We use them to address your request and necessary follow-up. Your email provider processes messages under its own policies.

Diagnostic export requires you to preview and explicitly share the file through the system share sheet. It is not uploaded automatically. The current export mainly contains its creation time, system platform, library revision and counts, source availability, import-batch identifiers, states and reason codes. It does not automatically attach whole audio files, external-source passwords or your complete library. Screenshots, system logs or backups you add yourself may contain more personal information; inspect them first.

These legal and support pages include no analytics scripts, tracking cookies or third-party fonts. Their hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information and request logs to serve the pages. When served by GitHub Pages, see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Webpage access is distinct from local library processing.

Playback, picker, scanner, database and system-sharing libraries implement their respective features; being a third-party component does not automatically make one a cloud recipient of your library. External file providers, servers, share destinations, page hosts and email providers may actually receive information according to the operations you choose. Use of third-party code does not remove the developer’s responsibilities.

Apart from requested transfers/connections and necessary service processing, we disclose only the minimum information we actually hold when required by applicable law, valid legal process or a legally justified need to protect life, safety or lawful rights. This does not give us access to a local library we do not hold.

11. Retention, deletion and backups

  • Local data: library records, settings and imported copies normally remain until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall. Operating-system backups and migration settings may retain or restore copies.
  • Different deletion actions: “Remove from library” primarily removes references and organization relationships. “Delete managed copy” removes the app-created audio copy and cannot be undone. Neither automatically deletes originals in other apps, external folders or other devices.
  • External connections: delete saved source settings or remove authorized folders in their management screens. Removing a configuration does not delete an account or logs on the external server.
  • Exported files: backups, migration archives, downloads and recipients’ copies are controlled by whoever holds them. Delete them separately in the relevant file manager, cloud drive or receiving device.
  • Support correspondence: messages and attachments are retained only as needed to address the request, follow up, resolve disputes or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion, subject to legally required retention and necessary records.

We have no cloud copy of your local media and cannot restore deleted songs, playlists or lyrics. Export and verify a separate backup before cleanup or migration.

Temporary session credentials cease to authorize access when a session ends, expires or is replaced; imported items remain subject to library retention rules. Transfers, imports and compatible playback may create temporary files or caches whose retention depends on cancellation, task completion and system cleanup. Leaving a room does not mean secure erasure of every file already created. Revoking permission or disabling a feature cannot delete a recipient’s copy.

Music currently has no registration, cloud account profile or account-deletion process. To stop using it, export files you need, then delete local content and the app, and separately remove external backups. Contact us to delete information sent to support. Deleting Music does not delete your Apple, Google, cloud-drive or WebDAV account. If support records must be legally retained, we will explain the basis and restrict other uses.

12. Access, correction, deletion and other rights

You can inspect, organize and export local data, remove records or managed copies, remove folders/sources, stop sharing and revoke permissions. We have no cloud copy and ordinarily cannot remotely read, delete or recover your files.

For support correspondence and other personal information we actually hold, applicable law may provide rights to access or know, correct, delete, obtain a portable copy, restrict, object, withdraw consent or appeal a denial. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing before it. GDPR/UK GDPR, applicable US state laws and Chinese personal-information law each have their own applicability rules and exceptions.

Email onebestai@gmail.com with “Music privacy request” in the subject. Describe your contact with us, the information involved and the request; do not attach passwords or a full library. We will seek only the identity or authorized-agent verification necessary, respond within applicable statutory time limits, and explain any lawful extension, verification problem or partial refusal and available appeal route. You may also complain to a competent data-protection authority or regulator.

Requests concerning recipients’ copies, system accounts, cloud drives or servers should go to the party actually controlling them. We do not promise to delete data held by independent services.

13. Children and younger users

Music is a general-purpose local media tool, not a service for building children’s profiles, child-directed advertising or collecting children’s information. Younger users should obtain parental guidance or permission required by applicable law before sharing, scanning, connecting externally or contacting support, and should not provide addresses, school details, passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Where parental consent is legally required, including Chinese rules giving special protection to personal information of children under 14 and relevant consent rules elsewhere, accepting general terms is not treated as valid parental consent. A parent who believes a child has supplied personal information to support may contact us for verification and appropriate deletion or handling. We request only necessary information to verify parental authority.
