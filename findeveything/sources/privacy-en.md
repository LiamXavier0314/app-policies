# Find Everything Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Policy explains how Find Everything (the “App”) handles information when you use its local search, import, preview, system integration, trial, and one-time purchase features. The developer and controller for information handled by us is Shenzhen Zhiding International Trade Co., Ltd. (深圳至鼎国际贸易有限公司). Contact us at [onebestai@gmail.com](mailto:onebestai@gmail.com). The [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.html) explain the service and purchase conditions.

## 1. Privacy at a glance

Your authorized source content, queries, extracted text, transcripts, and search vectors are processed and stored on your device for the App’s local features. We do not operate an App service that uploads those items to our servers for search or model training. This does not mean the App never uses a network: model downloads, fetching a web page you request, store and RevenueCat purchase verification, external links, system services, and explicit sharing can involve third parties.

There is no advertising or App usage-analytics feature enabled in the App. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use your local library to build an advertising profile. Purchase-verification records and messages you send to support are separate from local content and may be processed outside your device.

## 2. Scope and responsibility

This Policy covers our App processing, support communications, and these static legal pages. Apple, Google, RevenueCat, model hosts, web publishers, email providers, operating-system services, and apps you share to may handle information under their own policies. We identify those interactions below rather than describing every third party as processing solely on our behalf.

The App does not require a separate developer-operated account for ordinary local search. A store account may be needed for purchases or restoration. A RevenueCat-generated anonymous App user identifier is a technical identifier, not a guarantee that purchase records cannot be linked to a person by a store or another lawful source.

## 5. Network requests and third parties

**Apple and Google Play.** The applicable store processes payment, store-account authentication, approval, purchase history, fraud protection and refunds. We do not receive your complete card number or store password. Apple and Google can retain information under their own legal and operational requirements. Google Play purchase processing is relevant only when a supported Google Play version actually offers that purchase; this Policy does not claim it is enabled in every Android build.

**RevenueCat.** We use RevenueCat’s purchase SDK to configure available products, validate and cache store purchases, restore access, and receive current entitlement information. This involves an App user identifier and purchase-related technical and transaction data. We do not send it your local files, search queries, extracted text, transcripts or vectors for entitlement verification. SDK configuration, cache refresh, product loading and restoration can connect to the service; purchase traffic is not limited to the moment the purchase page is open. See [RevenueCat’s privacy information](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

**Model downloads.** If you download models, requests may go to Hugging Face or the configured hf-mirror host, including fallback when a source is unavailable. Those hosts and their delivery networks receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address and the requested model resource. Downloading a model does not require uploading your personal library to that host. Relevant provider information includes [Hugging Face’s Privacy Policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy); a separately operated mirror has its own practices. You can use a supported local model-import flow instead of a network download where offered.

**Web capture.** When you ask the App to fetch a web page, it contacts the supplied site, which can receive your IP address, requested URL, and ordinary request metadata. The site may process tracking parameters or authentication information included in that URL. The App cannot control the site’s policies or ensure every page is publicly accessible. Avoid submitting URLs containing secrets that are not needed for the operation.

**Operating system, external apps and support.** System permissions, optional system-search exposure, system backups, dictation, original providers and explicit sharing are governed in part by the OS and the service you choose. Sending an email gives its contents to the relevant mail providers and us. Opening an external link gives the destination an ordinary web request. We do not treat an external provider’s own collection as our promise that all information remains offline.

## 6. Seven-day trial, buyout, and purchase privacy

The free trial begins only when a supported claim succeeds and is verified. For the Apple free non-consumable trial product, the App reads its verified original purchase date and calculates 168 hours of access. It does not use your private search content to decide payment eligibility. The trial does not automatically renew or charge you. A separate valid non-consumable buyout grants the purchased App’s Pro access without a seven-day limit.

Both products may use an entitlement named “7-day”; the App checks the verified product identifier to distinguish them. Restoring a purchase or clearing local data does not reset the original trial. An arbitrary local “Pro” flag or a historical purchase alone is not sufficient proof of a current valid purchase. Refund or revocation state may be refreshed through the store and RevenueCat. Offline verified caches cannot instantly reflect every remote change.

Local data deletion is separate from store refunds and transaction-record deletion. We cannot erase a store’s purchase history from your device-cleanup action. Requests concerning store-held records should also be directed to the store under its available privacy procedures.

## 8. Retention

Local content copies, models, extracted text, vectors, indexes, grants and preferences remain until you remove them, clear App data, revoke relevant access where removal is supported, or the App rebuilds or replaces data for the requested feature. Original source files are controlled by their source provider and are not automatically deleted by clearing an index. Local failure records and processing state may persist to permit explicit retry and diagnosis. A library that remains only on your device is retained through the local controls described above rather than a server-side retention schedule.

Purchase records and verified caches may persist to support restoration, prevent repeated free claims, resolve disputes and satisfy store, RevenueCat and legal retention obligations. Their retention is governed by the relevant provider’s requirements and our actual relationship with that provider; we do not promise that local cleanup deletes those records everywhere. Support communications are kept as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve the issue, maintain relevant correspondence and comply with legal obligations, then deleted or minimized when no longer needed. Retention depends on the purpose, the category of information and the applicable legal requirements.

Operating-system or provider backups may retain copies under your device and cloud settings. Do not assume uninstalling deletes every backup or every externally shared copy. Manage those copies through the corresponding OS, cloud provider or receiving app.

## 10. Security

We use platform storage boundaries and available operating-system file protection, limit requested access to relevant features, and verify model integrity and purchase information where supported. The local SQLite database is not separately encrypted by the App. Device locks, OS security updates, trusted input sources and careful sharing remain important. No storage, network, local-model or verification mechanism can guarantee absolute security or resistance to every form of device compromise, clock manipulation or malicious file.

If we become aware of a personal-information incident for which we have notification duties, we will take appropriate steps and notify affected people or authorities as required by law. Do not email passwords, full card details or unnecessary private content to us.
